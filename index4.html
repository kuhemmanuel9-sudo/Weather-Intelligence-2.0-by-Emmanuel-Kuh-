About

Weather Intelligence is a live weather platform built by Emmanuel Kuh with Open Meteo, NOAA, Leaflet, and clean charting tools to turn forecast data into clear maps, charts, alerts, and planning guidance.

It brings current conditions, favorites, city comparisons, and weather analysis into one responsive page designed for everyday users and professionals.
